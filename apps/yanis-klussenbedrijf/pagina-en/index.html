To what we do

Houses

A house is renovated from bottom to top. Several floors, your own front door and usually no set of rules telling you what is allowed. And often you stay living in it.

  • Bathroom, toilet and kitchen completely renewed
  • Walls removed or added for a different layout
  • An attic or an extra bedroom finished off
  • Plastering, painting and floors throughout the house
More about houses

Apartments

Renovating an apartment is not the same as a detached house. You share walls with neighbours, the materials have to come up a stairwell or a lift, and the pipework is fixed in the riser.

  • Bathroom and toilet completely renewed
  • Kitchen out, new connections, kitchen in
  • Walls stripped, plastered and painted
  • New floor, working from the existing floor height
More about apartments

Shops and commercial space

In a shop something counts that does not come up in a home: every day the door stays shut costs money. So we talk about the schedule first and about the tiles after that.

  • Reshaping the space with new walls
  • Lighting and circuits for the new layout
  • Finishing floors, walls and ceilings
  • A pantry and toilet for staff and customers

# Kok Vastgoedonderhoud

Concept marketing site for a Dutch schilder en spuitwerk vakman based in Nieuwegein. The owner is a young craftsman whose calling card is spray finishing (latex- en lakspuiten) on doors, frames, ceilings, walls, and overhead doors, alongside conventional binnen- en buitenschilderwerk and wandafwerking.

The site exists because there is currently only a Wordpress stub. It needs to look more capable than the operation appears in followers (Instagram is a small but high-quality photo archive; only one Google review so far). Design has to do the trust work that volume metrics cannot.

## Register

**brand** — this is a marketing site. Design IS the product.

## Users

Two audiences, in priority order:

1. **Homeowners** in Nieuwegein, IJsselstein, Utrecht, Houten, and the Montfoort corridor planning a paint job. They want to feel that the person showing up at their front door will leave their home cleaner than they found it. Typical decision triggers: a fresh-built house with cracking walls, a tired front door, an outdated kitchen they want sprayed back to factory finish.
2. **Light commercial / property owners** with overhead doors, garages, and shopfronts to spray. Lower volume, higher-margin work.

Both audiences are Dutch-first. The English layer exists for expats but stays secondary.

## Product purpose

Convert "I should get someone in to look at this" into a submitted intake with photos, postcode, and a service category. Everything else (Werkwijze, Reviews, FAQ) exists to remove the friction between that thought and that submit click.

## Brand voice

- **Vakman, niet verkoper.** Concrete language about the job. "Strak in de lak" beats "premium finish". Names tools and brands (Sigma Coatings, Veveo Platina) where it adds credibility.
- **Honest scale.** Don't pretend to be a 20-year firm. The Werkwijze and pricing language admits "geen prijs zonder eerst gezien te hebben". Builds trust through small precise commitments instead of inflated proof.
- **Calm, modern.** No exclamation marks, no superlatives, no "trots", no marketing-speak. Sentences short, declarative, occasionally dry.
- **Dutch first, written like a Dutch contractor, not translated marketing English.**

## Anti-references

What this site must not look like:

- The cluttered Wordpress contractor template: hero photo + 6-pack icon grid + cookie banner.
- "Painters of [city]" SEO landing pages with stock images of paint cans.
- SaaS-cream / pastel-gradient lifestyle aesthetic. This is a trade.
- Aggressive primary-colour CTAs ("CALL NOW") and orange exclamation badges.
- Stock-photo "happy painter in white overalls" imagery. We use real Instagram archive only.

Closer to: an architect's portfolio page, an editorial magazine spread on a craftsman, a B-Corp manufacturing brand. Not a trade-services lead-gen template.

## Strategic principles

1. **Photos are proof.** The Instagram archive is the most valuable asset on the site. The Showcase has to read as a portfolio first, decoration second.
2. **One review is enough if it speaks plainly.** Quote the actual Dutch text. Don't pad with fake reviews. Don't average a single number.
3. **Latex- en lakspuiten is the differentiator.** Surface it in the Hero, in Services, in Pillars. It signals modern technique and explains the visual smoothness in the photos.
4. **Service area is a trust signal.** Naming Nieuwegein, IJsselstein, Utrecht, Houten, Montfoort makes it concrete and small enough to feel personal.
5. **Intake form > phone CTA.** A form with photos lets the owner triage from a phone before booking the survey. Phone link must exist but is secondary.

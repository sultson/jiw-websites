Website Build Instructions — Boutique Expat/VIP Relocation Site
1. Core positioning
Luxury boutique expat concierge — the only one of its kind in NL. Site must read as high-end, exclusive, personal.
Tone: American-style "over the top" storytelling, not Dutch sobriety. Target audience is wealthy internationals (US, Swiss, Russian, German, Mexican...), not Dutch locals.
Visual direction: keep the existing blue + gold, chic palette — client explicitly likes it. The failure was everything below the hero.
2. Site architecture — three-path structure

Homepage hero must immediately split visitors into three clearly distinct paths (reference: the US insurance-site pattern shown on the call):

Immigration services — single visa/permit services; pages named after high-search-volume service terms (these page names are SEO assets)
Expat packages + single services — relocation packages AND individually bookable services (housing, schooling, etc. — previous site omitted single services; must be restored). Corporate services included (multi-family corporate relocations).
VIP — artists, athletes, weekend fly-ins; imagery and copy must feel top-tier
Each path gets its own tailored contact form (custom code advantage — don't reuse one generic form)
Homepage should let users reach everything by scrolling (mobile behavior), while paths also exist as separate sections/pages
Her menu document is the leading source of truth — she rewrote the entire structure, added captions and extra info per page. Follow it exactly; if something can't be done, say so honestly.
3. Language & SEO (critical — this killed the last site)
English is the primary language. All content, all SEO, in English. No Dutch-primary anything.
Multilingual: pick ~top 5 client languages: so German, Dutch, French, Spanish, Russian; every language version must be a 1:1 mirror of the structure (previous site changed structure on language switch — unacceptable)
Language selection via polite prompt/banner ("You appear to be visiting from X — switch language?") — H&M-style. Never auto-force redirect by geo, it confuses Google.
4. Pricing presentation
Packages start ~€2,000 up to ~€10,000 — presentation must justify the price: context, explanation, what's included, luxury framing
Kill the alternating left/right/left "highway" layout. No image-text zigzag repeated down the page.
Every package/service block ends in a clear CTA. Never show a price with a dead end.
5. Contact & conversion
Primary channel: WhatsApp — persistent/floating WhatsApp button site-wide
Low-friction contact forms (short; long forms proven to kill conversion per Clarity data) — one per path, with path-relevant fields
NO booking calendar / Calendly — she will not maintain availability slots, and instant 24/7 personal response is part of the VIP promise
CTAs present throughout but not desperate/pushy — no homepage contact form ambush, no aggressive follow-you-everywhere widgets. Elegant, inviting.
She only needs ~1 lead/week — quality over volume in all conversion design
6. Legally required + trust elements (missing from current site)
Chamber of Commerce (KvK) number
Full contact details
Social media links
All her USPs prominently woven in: only boutique expat agency in NL, personal 24/7 approach, unique region, unique packages, embassy-world reputation
7. Content & media
She supplies everything: full text file, ~200 purchased images, menu/structure/instruction docs. Do not write content from scratch — design and execute what's provided.
Hero video: current one visibly stutters/restarts — replace with a smooth loop or better asset
No blog / no ongoing content burden for her — site must be turnkey and "done"

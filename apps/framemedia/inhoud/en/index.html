The difference

What is non-spot advertising?

A commercial lasts twenty seconds and sits outside the programme. A non-spot lasts minutes and is the programme. Those are not the same thing in a different size.

How it works

Why it works

It does not bore

The channels we broadcast on reach the whole of the Netherlands. The difference is not in the reach but in the form: an item the viewer stays seated for, instead of a break they zap past.

It does not bore

Unlike traditional television advertising, branded content aims to engage viewers in a natural way and bring them closer to a brand. That can be through an interview with the owner and a report about the brand and the business.

It makes a good match

The best branded content tells stories an audience gets enthusiastic about. From then on the consumer associates those emotions with the brand, which makes people remember it for longer.

It carries over to social

Branded content has real potential to go viral. It is presented in formats that can be shared, and on social media the right content often produces a snowball effect.

About us

Where content meets advertising.

FRAME MEDIA is the platform matching supply and demand for programme sponsorship — branded content — in television programmes. Those programmes mostly go out on weekend mornings and afternoons on the Dutch commercial channels.

What we do

By advertising in, or taking part in, the right programme on the right channel, a product or service reaches its intended audience quickly.

FRAME MEDIA specialises in sponsored editorial and advises and guides you, as a business owner, in finding television programmes in which your product or service reaches a large audience.

Instead of a commercial break lasting a few seconds, we deliver what is known as the non-spot principle, which runs on average between three and five minutes. It gives real attention to the products and services of the company in question. And the company’s representative — the owner, CEO or marketing manager — is interviewed by the presenter, on location or in the studio.

Why television

Television offers a great deal of scope to bring brands and products to attention locally, regionally and nationally. Whether it is communicating a company’s image, launching a new product, effectively building name recognition or developing a sponsored TV format: television is and remains a powerful medium in this changing media landscape.

We have been making television advertising since 1981.

The broadcast is of course accompanied by the various possibilities of social media, to generate still more traffic to the company, product or service. The television broadcast is the beginning, not the end.

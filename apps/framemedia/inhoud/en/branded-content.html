Six steps from the first conversation to the broadcast. You keep control: you decide the subject, and the running order is ready before the camera is switched on.

Step 4, post-production

Ready for broadcast means measured

An item is not finished when it looks good. Broadcast has hard requirements: the picture goes past the vectorscope, the audio level is read out and corrected. Only then is it ready to air. Colour grading, audio post, voice-over and the clearing of music rights are included in the price.

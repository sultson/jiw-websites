Preparation

Tips for being on television

Anyone standing in a studio for the first time usually underestimates three things. We send the note “Tips for appearing on television” in advance, but these are the main points.

Allow for make-up

Television make-up is not vanity but technique: studio light is merciless. Allow roughly 45 minutes for a woman and 15 minutes for a man, and build a schedule that works for everyone who appears on camera.

Think about your clothing

Not all clothing works on camera. Clothes with stripes or checks do not reproduce well: a fine pattern like that flickers on screen. Bring spare clothing, so there is something to choose from if one outfit does not work against the set or looks restless on camera.

Stop as little as possible

A conversation that runs through in one go sounds better than one that has been stitched together. Recording in separate parts is possible, but recording and editing time go up, and that is your money too.

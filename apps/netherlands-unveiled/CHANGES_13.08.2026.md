# What has changed on your website

netherlandsunveiled.com · August 2026

## What you asked for

**The video** sits in the About us section. A still picture with a play button, and the film starts when it is pressed.

**Ten stories from the old blog**, rewritten and expanded from the original notes, with your own photos. Each has its own page, opened by clicking a card.

**The long sentence at the top of the home page is gone**, along with the row of three boxes below it.

**Nijmegen has its own section** on the home page: the oldest city in the country, the only Dutch one built on two levels, the Stevenskerk and the Waalkade, with a photo of you and a group. It also has a page of its own.

**Keukenhof now comes before About us.**

**The section is called About us.** The old Amsterdam to New Amsterdam heading is gone, and so is the "About Marion" line above it.

**"Most of our tours are done by Marion."** opens that section.

**The contact section reads "Tell us what you want to see."**

**The three reviews are on the site** in the guests' own words: Hanna at CTN Tours, Luca Matteini from Italy, Lena from Sweden. Reviews is its own item in the menu, and the section sits below Keukenhof.

The old site's star ratings, 4.9 and 4.8, were not carried over. They were typed in by hand rather than given by guests, and Google can penalise a site for showing scores nobody gave.

**The flags open their own page**, listing each guiding language with a few words written in it. The Dutch flag stays in the header. There is no Dutch text in the list.

## Also changed

**French pages can now be found.** The site used to switch language with a button, which meant only the English version existed as far as Google was concerned. English and French are now separate pages, with French words in the French addresses.

**Every tour has its own page** in both languages: Amsterdam, Keukenhof, the villages, The Hague, Rotterdam, Delft, Nijmegen and Grand Holland. Someone searching for "Nijmegen walking tour" lands there directly instead of hunting through the home page.

Each tour page carries a summary panel: length, on foot or by coach, languages, how to book. All of it comes from your own descriptions. Where a length was never fixed it says set to the group. No prices anywhere, since none are published.

**One address instead of three.** The site was appearing at three web addresses at once, which split its standing with Google three ways. Everything now goes to netherlandsunveiled.com. Links shared earlier still work.

**Pages load faster**, since each one now carries a single language instead of both.

The site has 44 pages, up from 6.

- **ABOUT** (1): fb-015 — Jenny's own nails (blue glitter close-up, her FB caption: "op mijn eigen nagels gezet"). No true workspace/interior photo exists in the raw media; this is the most personal shot available. gmaps-007/fb-030 show the work station dust grid but are client work photos.
- **OG** (1): fb-014 — bubblegum-pink almond nails, high colour contrast, reads well at small social-card size. Distinct from hero.
- **LOGO**: fb-001 (1290x1290 "N" on coral).
- **Boomerang clips** (3, from MARQUEE-set sources): fb-002 (red), gmaps-003 (nude almond), fb-008 (lilac).

# Doelio — Product Context

## What it is
Doelio is a Dutch AI-consultancy. It helps companies pick the right AI tooling, cut the time and money they spend on manual work and expensive software subscriptions, and stay visible in a world where people increasingly ask AI instead of Google.

The website is a single-page marketing site. The only conversion is a WhatsApp conversation.

## Register
Brand. The design IS the product. A visitor's impression ("these people are technically sharp and current") is the thing being made.

## Users
Dutch business owners and operators (SME to mid-market). Practical, cost-aware, a little skeptical of AI hype, on the fence about whether AI is real for *their* business. Often reading on an iPhone in the evening. Secondary: international visitors (EN).

## Brand voice
Three words: **precise, luminous, calm.** Confident without hype. Concrete outcomes, not buzzwords. Plain Dutch. No jargon dumps, no "revolutionary AI synergy."

## What we do (the four areas)
1. **The right AI stack** — find the best combination of tooling: Claude / OpenAI ChatGPT; secure cloud models via Google & Microsoft; on-premise model setups; tailored models.
2. **Less time spent** — workflows for creating quotes (offertes) and ads; end-to-end AI-powered lead generation; voice agents and support agents.
3. **Lower tool costs** — self-maintainable internal tools that replace expensive subscriptions.
4. **Visible in the age of AI** — GEO (generative engine optimization), SEO tuned for AI answers.

## Anti-references
- Generic AI-startup landing page (neon-on-black, gradient blobs everywhere, "10x your workflow").
- The hero-metric template (big number + stats).
- Identical icon-title-text card grids.
- Hype copy, em dashes, filler.

## Strategic principles
- Apple "Liquid Glass" as the design language, done faithfully, not as decorative blur.
- Flawless on mobile and desktop. Mobile Safari is the primary target.
- Restraint: one strong idea per fold, lots of air.

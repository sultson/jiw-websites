Questions

What people ask about the bathroom

How long can I not shower?

A bathroom is a couple of weeks of work, and for part of that you cannot use it. Exactly how long depends on the room and on what has to happen to the pipework. We tell you up front and we plan it in one run, so nothing sits still for a week.

Do we have to move out?

Almost nobody does. We cover the walking route, keep the dust inside the bathroom as much as possible and clear up at the end of the day. If you only have one toilet, say so when we measure up and we take that into account in the planning.

Do we buy the sanitary ware ourselves or do you?

Either works. We buy at tile and flooring shops and have a network of suppliers, so if we do the work you buy along with us. If you have already picked something out or have it standing somewhere, we fit that just as happily.

Do you also do just part of the bathroom?

Yes. Only the tiling, only a new shower or only replacing the sanitary ware is fine too. That you can leave all of it with us does not mean you have to.

What does a bathroom cost?

That depends on the size of the room, on what has to come out and on what you choose. Two bathrooms of the same size can be far apart. The three packages show what is in there in any case, so you know what you are comparing. Those guide prices are work and materials together, VAT included. We only put the amount for your bathroom next to it once we have seen the room.

Are the materials included in a package price?

Yes. A package price with us is work and materials together, VAT included. The tiles, the sanitary ware, the taps and the small materials run through our buying and are not charged on top afterwards. We come along to the shop to choose, and what you pick counts towards the amount you are given. If you pick something dearer than what the package lists, you see on paper exactly what that difference is. If you have already bought tiles or sanitary ware yourself, we fit that just as happily and it comes off the amount.

Do I have to choose a package?

No. The packages are there so you can see what normally belongs and what does not, because that is where people come unstuck with quotes. If you want Complete with underfloor heating but without the lowered ceiling, that is simply what you get. We price what you want, not a box.

Can the layout be different from how it is now?

Usually yes. The shower somewhere else or turning the toilet around is a matter of moving pipework, and we do that ourselves. The waste is where the limit sits: it has to keep its fall. When we measure up we tell you straight away what can and cannot be done.

Do you speak English on site?

Yes. You can call, WhatsApp or email us in English, and the quote comes to you in English. On site the day-to-day is a mix of Dutch and English, so if something is not clear, say it and we will go through it again.

Bathroom due for replacement?

One line about your room is enough. We call you back and come and measure. A request commits you to nothing.

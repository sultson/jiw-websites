Where it comes unstuck

A tile is only as straight as the wall underneath

Most crooked tiling does not come from the tiler but from what is behind it. A bowed wall, a floor that falls away or a background that is still moving: you can set whatever you like, but it will not come out straight.

So we look at the background first. Making it flat, levelling it or plastering it first is part of the work, not extra work that surfaces halfway through. Only once it is flat does the first tile go on.

  • Setting out first where the full tiles land, so you get no slivers in view.
  • Levelling clips with large formats, so no edge stands proud.
  • Grout in one colour and one width, and the corners in silicone or a trim.
  • On floors we allow for movement joints, otherwise a long run cracks later.

Questions

What people ask about tiling

Can you tile over the old tiles?

Sometimes it can be done, but usually we advise against it. You lose room, you do not know what state the old layer is in, and if it lets go it takes the new one with it. We look at what is underneath when we measure up and then say whether it is sensible.

How many tiles should I order?

Always a bit more than the bare area, because there is cutting waste, and with herringbone or a large format there is more of it. We work it out for you once we have measured up. Keep a few tiles over for later too, because the same batch is often no longer available a year on.

Do you also do herringbone and other patterns?

Yes. Herringbone, brick bond, stack bond or a strip of mosaic in a niche. A pattern costs more time and more cutting waste than laying straight, so we say that up front rather than letting it be a surprise afterwards.

Can underfloor heating go under the tiling?

Yes, and tiles are the best floor for it: stone passes the heat on well. We lay the underfloor heating and the tiling ourselves, so the build-up and the adhesive are matched to it.

What does tiling cost?

That depends on the format, the pattern, the state of the background and how much cutting is needed. That is why there is no amount on this page. We measure up and you get a price on paper, with the material listed separately.

Questions

What people ask us beforehand

Can we deal with you in English?

Yes. You can call, WhatsApp or email in English and we answer in English, and the quote comes to you in English as well. The invoice and any warranty paperwork from suppliers is usually Dutch, because that is how it arrives here; ask and we will talk you through what it says.

Do you have a showroom?

No. We come to you to measure up. For choosing materials we go along to the shop where we buy, so you see the material for real.

Can I buy the material through you?

Yes. We buy as a business at tile and flooring shops and from suppliers. If we do the work, you can take the material through us. It is not compulsory: if you have already bought something, we simply fit that.

Do I have to collect material or be home for the delivery?

No. If the material runs through us, we arrange collection and make sure it is there on the day we start.

Do you also do just the boiler?

Yes. We fit boilers and service them afterwards, including a boiler we did not hang ourselves.

What does a bathroom or a floor cost?

That depends on the size of the room, what has to come out and what you pick. The same bathroom can end up twice as expensive on the tile choice alone. That is why there is no amount on this page: you get a price after we have measured up.

Can I have just one single job done?

Yes. Only the tiling, only a floor or only the painting is fine too.

Shall we come round?

One line about your job is enough. We call you back and arrange a time to come and look.

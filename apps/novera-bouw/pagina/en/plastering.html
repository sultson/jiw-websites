Why it matters

Paint hides nothing, paint shows it up

A wall that is not flat only really shows once it is painted. Raking light from a window pulls out every bump and every seam, and with a silk finish it only gets worse. What you are looking at then is not a paint fault but a background that is not right.

So with us plastering is not an in-between step to be rushed. We take off the loose material, fill the holes, put on a primer where it is needed and then pull the wall smooth in layers.

  • Old walls: first look at what has to come off and what can stay.
  • New walls and plasterboard: seams filled and taken across the whole wall.
  • External corners get a bead, so nothing chips out later.
  • If you want to paper or tile afterwards, we work towards that finish.

Smooth plaster or textured

Questions

What people ask about plastering

How long does plaster need to dry?

Longer than people think, and it depends on the thickness, the time of year and whether the room can be ventilated. We say so per job and we will not paint over it while it is still wet in the wall, because it comes back later if we do.

Can we stay living in the house?

Yes, but the room being worked on is out of use for a few days and it is damp in there. We cover the floor and the walking route and keep the dust in that one room as much as we can.

Can plaster go over wallpaper or over a sprayed finish?

Over wallpaper never: it lets go and takes the plaster with it. Over an old sprayed finish sometimes, if it is sound. We tap and scrape it first and then say whether it has to come off. That saves you a wall that starts bulging after six months.

Do you also do a single wall?

Yes. One room, one ceiling or one wall that needs closing up after a renovation is fine. You do not have to order a whole house alongside it.

What does plastering cost?

That depends on the state of the background, on the finish you want and on how much has to come off first. A smooth wall on new plasterboard is different work from an old wall that has to be stripped first. We come and look and you get a price on paper afterwards.

Questions

What people ask about the toilet

How long can we not use the toilet?

A couple of days, depending on what has to come out and how the tiling runs. If you only have one toilet in the house, say so when we measure up. We then plan it as tightly back to back as we can and tell you exactly which days it is out of action.

Can a floor-standing toilet become a wall-hung one?

Usually yes. A frame goes against the wall to carry the weight, the waste is rerouted and the whole lot gets boxed in. You lose a few centimetres of depth and you get a clear floor in return. We check when we measure up whether the wall allows it.

Do the tiles have to go up to the ceiling?

That is a choice, not a requirement. Fully tiled is the easiest to keep clean. Half tiled with plaster or paint above is quieter and saves on material. We do both and show you what works out better in your room.

Do you also just replace the pan?

Yes. Only a new pan, only a leaking cistern or only a new flush plate is fine too. You do not have to order a renovation along with it.

What does a toilet renovation cost?

That depends on what has to come out, on the size of the room and on the tile you pick. That is why there is no amount on this page. We come and look and you get a price on paper afterwards, with the material listed separately.

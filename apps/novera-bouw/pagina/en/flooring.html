Where it goes wrong

A floor stands or falls on what lies beneath it

Almost everything people have trouble with later is not in the planks themselves. A floor that creaks, a joint that opens up or a plank that lifts: that comes from a subfloor that was not flat, from too little room along the wall or from damp out of a concrete floor.

So we check the floor first and see whether it is flat and dry enough. If it is not, we level it first. That is a day extra and it saves you a floor you have to have laid again in two years.

  • Underlay matched to your subfloor: sound, damp and insulation.
  • The same expansion gap all along the wall, so the floor can move.
  • Skirting and threshold profiles included, at doors and at the stairs too.
  • We take the offcuts and the old floor away ourselves.

02 — Vinyl

Vinyl, click or glued down

Vinyl copes with water and feels warmer underfoot than laminate, so in a kitchen, hallway or bathroom it is often the better choice. Glued down it is quieter and it is the best combination with underfloor heating; click is quicker and can come out again later.

Questions

What people ask about floors

Laminate or vinyl, which is better for me?

Roughly: laminate is cheaper and there is plenty of choice, vinyl copes with water and feels warmer. In a hallway, kitchen or bathroom we advise vinyl. In a bedroom laminate is fine. We look at the room with you when we measure up and at how you use it.

Can it go over underfloor heating?

Yes, but not every floor is suitable for it and not every underlay lets the heat through. We lay underfloor heating ourselves, so we match the build-up and the floor choice to each other instead of landing you with two firms.

Does the old floor have to come out?

Usually yes. We take it out and cart it away. If there is something flat and firmly fixed, it can sometimes go over the top, but then your floor rises and doors have to be trimmed. We check that on site.

Will you lay a floor I bought myself?

Yes. We do check whether the package is complete: underlay, skirting and profiles often get forgotten. Whatever is missing we bring along.

What does a floor cost?

That depends on the area, the pattern, the underlay and whether levelling is needed. That is why there is no amount on this page. We measure up and you get a price on paper, with the material listed separately.

Our work

Bathroom renovation

The bathroom is our trade

A bathroom is the hardest room in the house to renovate. Strip-out, pipework, electrics, tiling and sanitary ware all come together in a few square metres, and every step has to fit the one before it. Split that over four firms and you are always waiting for the next one.

With us the same team does all of it. The one laying the pipe already knows where the tile is going, and there is one person you can call when something is not right.

  • Three packages, so you see up front what is included and what is not.
  • We measure up at your home, and only then put a price on paper.
  • Tiles and sanitary ware through our buying, or you supply them yourself.

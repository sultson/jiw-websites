How you spot it

The edges are where you see who did it

Almost anyone can roll a wall. The difference sits in the transitions: the line where the wall meets the ceiling, the edge along a frame, the corner behind the radiator. That is where you see at a glance whether time went into the preparation.

We mask those edges, sand and prime whatever needs priming and work in layers. That costs a day more than rolling straight over it, and it is exactly what you will be looking at for the next ten years.

  • Bare or repaired patches primed first, otherwise the paint flashes.
  • Wood sanded and degreased, and bare spots put into primer.
  • Two coats where they are needed, not one thick one that will sag.
  • At the end the tape comes off, we clear up and leave the room usable.

Questions

What people ask about painting

Do we have to buy the paint ourselves?

You do not have to. We bring it along and bill it separately, so you can see what the work costs and what the material costs. If you already have paint standing there or want a particular colour from a particular range, we simply work with that.

Can we stay living in the house?

Yes. We work room by room, cover up and clear away at the end of the day. The room we are working in you cannot use that day, the rest of the house you can.

Do you paint outside as well?

This page is about interior painting, because that is what we do most. If you have something outside, put it to us and we will say honestly whether we take it on.

Does wallpaper have to come off before you start?

Usually yes. Painting over wallpaper always shows up in the seams, and if it lets go later it takes the paint with it. We take it off and get the wall right first, or we plaster it if it is still not flat after that.

What does painting cost?

That depends on the number of rooms, the state of the background and how much woodwork is involved. An empty house is different work from a lived-in room full of furniture. That is why there is no amount on this page: we come and look and you get a price on paper.

Due a fresh coat?

One line about what needs painting is enough. We call you back and come and look.
